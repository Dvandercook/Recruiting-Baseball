Staff Sharing & Sync

Sign in and this board is the same board on every coach's phone, iPad and laptop. Your work always saves here first — sync happens after, and keeps working when the wifi doesn't.

Coaches don't sign themselves up. You add each one in the Supabase dashboard under Authentication → Users, and give them the password you set.

Paste A List Of Programs

One per line. A number at the front is read as the rank — 1. Canes National — otherwise you can use the order they're in. Programs already on your list keep their tier and just get the rank.

Paste Profile Links

Drop in Perfect Game or PBR profile addresses — one per line, as many as you like. A PBR address carries the player's name and state, so those match themselves. A Perfect Game address is only an id, so you'll pick the player.

Read A Written Book

Photograph the pages of the blank book after you've written on them. The handwriting gets transcribed onto each player's notes, and any velo, pop, 60 or EV you wrote down becomes a measurable.
